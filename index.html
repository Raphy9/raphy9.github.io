About

I work on the formal specification and verification of distributed systems, in particular systems that must keep working when some of their participants behave arbitrarily (Byzantine faults).

At CEA-List, I am in charge of the formal verification side of Ledgera, an open-source distributed ledger that certifies computations shared between parties who do not trust each other. I write the formal specification of its behaviour and derive from it the tests run against its Rust implementation. I do my master's as an apprentice, alternating between the university and CEA-List, under the supervision of Álvaro García Pérez.

Research interests

  • Formal specification and deductive verification
  • Byzantine fault-tolerant protocols and distributed ledgers
  • Specification-based testing
  • Verification of Rust programs
